Everything You Need to Master Any Subject

Cornote provides all the tools for effective, focused studying

Instant PDF Processing

Upload any PDF and get instant text extraction. Watch as Cornote transforms your lecture notes into organized study material.

AI-Generated Questions

Cornote uses advanced AI to generate 5-10 thoughtful study questions that test your understanding of key concepts.

Cornell Note System

Study using the proven Cornell note-taking method. Questions, notes, and summary all on one beautiful, distraction-free page.

Instant Answer Grading

Get immediate feedback on your answers. Cornote grades your responses and explains what you got right and where you can improve.

Summary Feedback

Write your summary and get intelligent feedback showing which key concepts you covered and which ones you missed.

Focus Timer (Pomodoro)

Auto-Save & Revise

All your answers, grades, and feedback are automatically saved. Come back anytime to review or revise your work.

Track Your Progress

See your grading progress at a glance. Know exactly which concepts you've mastered and which need more review.
